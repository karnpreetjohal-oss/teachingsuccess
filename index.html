How much does private tuition cost?

Prices start from £15 per hour. Exact pricing depends on year group, subject and level. Call 07909 274901 for a tailored quote.

Do you have after-school and weekend availability?

Yes. We offer after-school, evening and weekend slots, subject to current availability. Booking a free trial is the quickest way to secure your preferred time.

Which areas do you cover?

We support families across Smethwick, Birmingham, Oldbury, Bearwood, West Bromwich, Bloxwich, Willenhall, Wolverhampton, Walsall and Manchester. We also teach online for families anywhere in the UK.

Do you have a maths teacher in Smethwick?

Yes. Teaching Success offers teacher-led maths tuition in Smethwick for KS2, KS3, GCSE and A-Level students. Start with the maths teacher guide or book a free trial.

Yes for school English and GCSE English Language. We support reading, writing, SPaG, GCSE English Language and Literature; we do not advertise modern foreign language lessons.

Yes. Bloxwich, West Bromwich and Willenhall families can use local Walsall or Smethwick tutor routes, or online tuition when travel is not convenient.

Do you offer online and in-person tutoring?

Yes. You can choose online lessons, in-person lessons, or a blended approach depending on your child’s needs and your family schedule.

Is the first lesson really free?

Yes. We offer a free 1-hour trial lesson and consultation so you can check fit, teaching style and next steps before committing.

Who teaches the lessons?

Lessons are taught by qualified teachers including Kevin Johal (secondary, science, A-Level), Serena Johal (primary, English, 11+), Mr Olu (secondary — Wolverhampton), Mr Vasta (Maths, Science, English — Walsall) and Miss Kay (secondary — Manchester). See our full tutor profiles.

How long is each tutoring session?

Most sessions are 60 minutes so there is enough time for teaching, guided practice and feedback in one lesson.

How do you track student progress?

We track progress through lesson feedback, topic mastery checks and regular review points so parents can clearly see what has improved and what comes next.
