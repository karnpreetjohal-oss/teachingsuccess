Practical guide · Updated 19 September 2026

Match the question to the teacher before matching the diary

A convenient time is only useful if the tutor can support the learner’s subject and stage. Start with the question the learner needs help answering.

A practical example

For a primary reading concern, describe what happens when the child reads and retells a short passage. For a secondary calculation or exam task, include the course, level and a recent attempt. Read Kevin and Serena’s profiles, then confirm who is available for the specific need.

Ask what information would help them plan a first session and how they would review the starting goal. The profiles introduce the teachers; they do not replace a direct conversation about subject coverage, practical arrangements or any support the learner may require.
