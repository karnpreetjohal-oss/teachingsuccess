Test the parent’s journey before you launch your profile

A tutor business setup is not finished when the page looks attractive. A parent needs to understand the offer, find a relevant next step and know what happens after making contact.

A practical example

Ask someone unfamiliar with your plans to use the page on a phone. Can they identify the subjects, age groups, format and service area? Can they find the enquiry option without searching through a long biography? Use a clearly labelled test enquiry only when you have agreed how it will be handled.

During setup planning, agree what the website, social profiles and handover will include, who controls the accounts and which ongoing costs are separate. A launch package helps establish a usable presence; it does not guarantee search rankings, leads or student bookings.

Before you begin

Your questions, answered.

How much does Tutor Launch cost?

We provide a fixed-price quote after discussing your needs. It sets out the deliverables, any third-party costs, payment terms and timescale before you decide to proceed. Sending an enquiry does not commit you to buying anything.

Do I need to be an existing Teaching Success tutor?

No. The setup service is for teachers and experienced tutors establishing or refreshing their own independent business. It is separate from our tutor application and introduction process.

Will you find students for me?

Student introductions are not included in this package. We help you build a professional presence and an enquiry process; we do not guarantee enquiries, students, income or search rankings. Buying setup support is not a condition of receiving introductions.

Who owns the website and social accounts?

You retain ownership of your business accounts and the agreed business assets. Accounts should be registered in your name, with access provided securely for setup. Any third-party licences and recurring costs will be explained in your quote.

Does the package cover safeguarding, insurance or tax?

We can provide a practical checklist and signpost professional guidance, but the package is not legal, safeguarding, insurance or tax advice. You remain responsible for the requirements relevant to your tutoring business.

Can you support me after launch?

Optional website maintenance or content creation can be discussed and quoted separately. You are free to manage your business yourself or use another provider.
