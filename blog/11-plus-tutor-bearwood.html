Grammar School Targets

The schools Bearwood children aim for — and what they require.

Bearwood's B67/B68 location sits neatly between some of Birmingham's most sought-after grammar schools. Here is what each school looks for and how Teaching Success prepares for each one.

First choice

King Edward's Five Ways

Mixed grammar in Bartley Green — the closest KE school to Bearwood. Typically the most popular target for B67/B68 families. Strong all-round school with excellent results. Entry requires consistent performance across all four GL Assessment sections.

Popular target

KE Camp Hill Boys

Boys' grammar in Kings Heath. Strong science and maths tradition. Popular with Bearwood boys who want a mixed-gender catchment area within the KE family. Competitive entry — children need to perform well across all four sections.

Popular target

KE Camp Hill Girls

Girls' grammar in Kings Heath, adjacent to Camp Hill Boys. Often listed by Bearwood girls alongside Five Ways. Academically demanding — strong on humanities and sciences. Entry at a similar level of competition to Camp Hill Boys.

Girls

King Edward VI Girls' (Edgbaston)

The flagship girls' grammar — very high entry benchmark. Girls from Bearwood who perform in the top 5% nationally are encouraged to include this. Teaching Success ensures the preparation is thorough enough to make this a genuine rather than hopeful application.

Boys

Handsworth Grammar (Boys)

Independent of the KE foundation and popular with Bearwood boys as a strong achievable target. Located in Handsworth, accessible from Bearwood. A respected school that consistently achieves excellent GCSE and A-Level results.

Girls

KE Handsworth (Girls)

Girls' grammar in Handsworth Wood. A common first or second choice for Bearwood girls given its location. Competitive entry but achievable with consistent Year 5 preparation. Strong community and academic reputation.

Our Approach

How Teaching Success prepares Bearwood children for the 11+.

The preparation programme is the same for all children — but the pace, emphasis and school targets adapt to each child's starting point.

1

Diagnostic first lesson

The free trial lesson is also a diagnostic. Kevin or Serena works through a short section of a GL Assessment paper with your child — not to test them, but to identify exactly where they are strong and where the gaps are. You receive an honest assessment at the end, with a specific plan.

2

One-to-one weekly sessions

One hour per week, per subject area. Kevin covers maths and non-verbal reasoning. Serena covers English and verbal reasoning. Most families start with one subject and add a second as they progress. Sessions always include timed practice sections using real GL Assessment papers.

3

Mock tests to measure progress

Full four-section mock tests at our Smethwick centre — £5 per sitting. Auto-marked with a section breakdown and written teacher feedback. We recommend sitting one every two to three months from Year 5 to track progress against the entry benchmarks of your target schools.

FAQ

Common questions from Bearwood families.

Which grammar schools do Bearwood families typically target?

Most commonly King Edward's Five Ways, KE Camp Hill Boys and Girls, KE Handsworth (Girls) and Handsworth Grammar (Boys). High-achieving children also apply to KE Boys and KE Girls in Edgbaston. All use GL Assessment format papers.

How far is Teaching Success from Bearwood?

Our Smethwick centre is approximately 1–2 miles from Bearwood High Street — typically under 10 minutes by car. One of the shortest journeys of any family we serve.

What is the typical entry benchmark for KE Five Ways?

Entry benchmarks vary by year and cohort. Generally, children need to perform in the top 15–20% of GL Assessment entrants for a strong Five Ways application. Teaching Success tracks each child's practice paper scores against these benchmarks and gives honest guidance on where they stand.

Do you run weekend mock tests for Bearwood children?

Yes. Weekend £5 mock tests at our Smethwick centre — easily accessible from Bearwood. Full four-section Birmingham grammar school format, auto-marked with written teacher feedback. Weekend morning slots available. Use the mock test page to book.
