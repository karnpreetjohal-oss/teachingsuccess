Why touch typing is worth teaching properly

Typing is now a daily skill for schoolwork, exams that use laptops, coursework, applications and almost every office job — yet it is barely taught anywhere. Learners who type by sight stay slow, tire quickly and lose their thread mid-sentence. A structured course fixes the habit permanently, usually in weeks.

The programme is designed and led by a qualified teacher with an MEd in Educational Technology and Learning Design, so practice is sequenced the way skills are actually built — small, accurate steps before speed.

How the course is structured

  • Weeks 1–2 — posture, home row and finger discipline; accuracy over speed from the first session
  • Weeks 3–4 — full alphabet, common words and real sentences without looking down
  • Weeks 5–6 — numbers, punctuation and capitals; speed building begins once accuracy holds
  • Beyond — timed real-world tasks: schoolwork, emails and documents at a fluent, sustainable pace

Short daily practice between sessions (10–15 minutes) is set and reviewed each week, so progress is measured rather than hoped for.

For school-age children and students

Fluent typing quietly upgrades everything else: homework takes less effort, extended writing grows because hands keep up with ideas, and secondary students meet coursework and computer-based tasks with one less obstacle. For students who may use a laptop in exams, fluent typing is essential groundwork.

For adults

Adults who type with two fingers usually underestimate the cost: a working day contains hours of typing, and doubling your speed pays back permanently. The adult course uses the same structure at a faster pace, often alongside Microsoft Office training for a complete workplace-skills upgrade.

Mid-July 2026: the ideal summer skill

Touch typing is arguably the single best summer-holiday skill: it needs only a few weeks of consistent short practice, shows measurable progress (words per minute) that keeps motivation high, and lands just in time for the new school year or a September job push. A course started in mid-July is comfortably fluent before autumn.
