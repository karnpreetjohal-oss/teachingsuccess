What strong science tuition in Smethwick should include

Strong science teaching should do more than reteach content. It should show students how to use the content in explanations, calculations, practical questions and longer exam answers. That means combining subject knowledge with method, precision and practice under realistic conditions.

  • Clear diagnosis of topic gaps instead of vague “weak at science” labels
  • Teaching that separates knowledge, method and exam technique
  • Regular recall and spaced review across Biology, Chemistry and Physics
  • Targeted support for calculations, practical questions and longer responses
