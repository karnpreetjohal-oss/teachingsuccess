GCSE Science in Wolverhampton is often where students feel least confident. Three separate subjects, required practicals, 6-mark questions, and two tiers — it is a lot to manage. Mr Olu, a qualified secondary school teacher, simplifies the content into manageable chunks and builds students up to exam-ready performance.

Combined Science vs Triple Science

Most Wolverhampton secondary school students sit Combined Science (also called Double Award), which covers Biology, Chemistry and Physics in two GCSEs. Mr Olu supports both Combined Science and Triple Science students — the key difference is depth of content, not the structure of lessons.

What GCSE Science lessons with Mr Olu cover

  • Cell biology, genetics, evolution, ecology (Biology)
  • Atomic structure, bonding, rates of reaction, organic chemistry (Chemistry)
  • Forces, energy, waves, electricity, magnetism (Physics)
  • Required practicals — method, variables, data analysis
  • 6-mark extended answer technique — how to structure a full-mark response
  • Command words: describe, explain, evaluate, compare
  • Past papers and mark scheme practice

Where Students Lose the Most Marks in Science

The most common places GCSE Science students lose marks are: not explaining the mechanism (not just the fact), weak required practical responses, and poor use of scientific vocabulary in 6-mark questions. Mr Olu specifically targets these areas in sessions.
