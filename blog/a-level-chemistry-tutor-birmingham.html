Mid-August 2026: make Chemistry transition work before September

Incoming Year 12 students should test the GCSE foundations that A-Level Chemistry immediately assumes; incoming Year 13 students should use Year 12 evidence to identify the topic that restricts the most later work. Recent leavers should begin with their school or college’s post-results advice.

  • Check quantitative chemistry: Sample moles, formulae, equations and unit conversions before adding more advanced calculations.
  • Audit organic foundations: Make sure naming, functional groups and reaction conditions are organised rather than memorised as isolated facts.
  • Use practical evidence: Review variables, uncertainties and method evaluation alongside content questions.
  • Plan the independent week: Put one retrieval block and one problem-solving block around the school timetable before assignments build up.
