What Oldbury families tend to look for

The most consistent demand from Oldbury comes from secondary school families, particularly for GCSE support. English, Maths and combined Science are the subjects that come up most often, usually from Year 9 onwards when mock preparation starts to feel urgent. Primary families in Oldbury also use Teaching Success for Year 2 and Year 6 SATs preparation, and for 11+ prep targeting Sandwell and Birmingham grammar school places.

How Sandwell's school system shapes tuition needs

Sandwell has a mixed secondary landscape — some pupils attend local academies while others pursue grammar school places at schools in the wider West Midlands area. For families aiming at selective schools, 11+ preparation needs to start no later than Year 5 to allow enough time to build speed and accuracy. For those in non-selective secondary, GCSE success depends heavily on the quality of revision in Years 10 and 11 — which is where targeted one-to-one sessions make the most difference.

In-person versus online for Oldbury students

  • In-person: practical for families within easy reach of Smethwick, good for younger pupils and those who benefit from face-to-face interaction
  • Online: suits busy schedules, older secondary students, and families who want to avoid travel in school-night slots
  • Blended: some families use in-person during term time and switch to online during busy periods — both formats are available
