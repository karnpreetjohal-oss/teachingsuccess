What Year 4 tutoring should focus on

Good Year 4 tutoring should strengthen tables recall, comprehension, arithmetic accuracy and confidence with longer written answers. When those basics improve now, later KS2 feels much less rushed.

What Year 4 sessions at Teaching Success cover

  • Multiplication Tables Check preparation — building all tables to reliable timed recall before the Year 4 assessment
  • Four-digit place value and written arithmetic — column addition, subtraction, short multiplication and short division
  • Reading comprehension — identifying main ideas, retrieving details and beginning to infer from text
  • Writing in paragraphs — using fronted adverbials, inverted commas for speech and developing ideas across multiple sentences

The MTC: what it is and how Teaching Success prepares for it

The Multiplication Tables Check (MTC) is a statutory government assessment taken in June of Year 4. It tests all times tables from 1 to 12 in a 25-question online assessment with 6 seconds per question. Unlike in-class tests, the MTC requires speed as well as accuracy. Teaching Success sessions build times table fluency table-by-table, starting with the ones most commonly tested (6, 7, 8 and 9), and include timed practice runs that replicate the real check environment so children feel prepared rather than surprised.

What parents should look for in the first four weeks

  1. Week 1: Serena assesses which times tables need the most work and agrees a practice plan with you.
  2. Week 2: Your child begins daily 5-minute table practice at home with the specific tables from the session.
  3. Week 3: Speed on the target tables is visibly improving — test them on a times table app to check.
  4. Week 4: First full progress update from Serena, including which tables are secure and which still need daily practice.
