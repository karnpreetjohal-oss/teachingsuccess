What Year 11 Maths covers

Year 11 is all about the summer GCSE exams. The focus moves from learning new content to full-course revision, past papers, and exam technique across the two (Foundation) or three (Higher) papers, with mock season in the winter acting as a key checkpoint. Higher students also secure vectors, circle theorems and functions.

Where Year 11 students most often need help

By Year 11 the issue is usually not a single topic but breadth and timing: retaining the whole specification, spotting which method a question wants, and not losing method marks under pressure. Mock results often reveal exactly where the marks are leaking.

The assessment that shapes Year 11

Mock exams in the winter are the key checkpoint before the summer GCSEs, and they drive predicted grades used for college and sixth-form applications. We use mock papers to target revision precisely at the topics losing the most marks.

What a weekly Year 11 Maths plan looks like

  • Structured revision across the whole specification, prioritised by mark value
  • Past-paper practice under timed conditions with detailed mark-scheme feedback
  • Targeting the specific topics a student's mocks show are weak
  • Grade-boundary awareness and technique for the final push

How lessons work

Every student starts with a free trial lesson so we can see exactly where the GCSE exam year gaps are, using recent schoolwork or a short diagnostic. From there, weekly one-hour sessions mix direct teaching on the current priority with worked examples, independent practice and a short review of earlier topics so nothing slips. Lessons are taught by qualified teachers in Smethwick and Birmingham, or online across the UK, from £15 an hour.

Looking ahead

Whether the goal is a grade 4/5 pass or a grade 8/9, the plan is built around the student's current marks and target grade from the first session.
