GCSE Maths tuition in Walsall

GCSE Maths is where structured tuition has the highest measurable impact on grades. Mr Vasta's GCSE sessions focus on:

  • Topic diagnosis from recent mock papers and school assessments
  • Foundation and Higher tier — AQA, Edexcel and OCR
  • Reteaching weak topics before moving to exam practice
  • Method marks and clear working — picking up partial credit
  • Calculator and non-calculator paper skills
  • Timed past papers every 3–4 sessions to build exam confidence

KS3 Maths — Year 7, 8 & 9

The habits and foundations built at KS3 determine how difficult GCSE becomes. Mr Vasta's KS3 sessions focus on arithmetic fluency, early algebra, fractions, ratio and problem-solving — the topics that cause the most difficulty in Year 10 when left unaddressed.
