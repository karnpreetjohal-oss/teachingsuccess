Explain a negative answer with a number line

Online maths support for Walsall learners can use a visible number line to distinguish a negative value from the operation of subtraction. The two signs can look similar while doing different jobs.

A practical example

Start at 3 and subtract 7 to reach −4. Compare −3 + 7, which reaches 4. Ask the pupil to identify the starting number, direction and distance before calculating. Then explain why subtracting a negative number reverses the direction.
