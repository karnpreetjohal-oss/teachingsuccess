Private Tutor in Quinton

Quinton (B32) sits on the western edge of Birmingham, bordering Sandwell and Worcestershire. It is slightly further from Smethwick than areas like Oldbury or Bearwood, but well connected by road — and for most Quinton families, online tuition is the more practical option for regular weekly sessions without travel time eating into the evening.

Subjects and year groups from Quinton

Quinton is a largely residential area with a mix of primary and secondary families. Demand from this area tends to cover primary maths and English for KS1 and KS2 pupils, 11+ preparation for Year 5 and Year 6 students targeting grammar schools in Birmingham and the wider West Midlands, and GCSE support for Years 10 and 11. A-Level requests from Quinton are less frequent but do come through, usually for Biology, Chemistry or Maths.

Online tuition for Quinton families

Because Quinton families are a little further from the Smethwick base, most opt for online sessions — which Kevin and Serena run in the same structured format as in-person lessons. A shared digital workspace means worksheets, past paper questions and written workings can all be done in real time on screen. For younger pupils, Serena keeps online sessions active and varied to maintain focus across the full hour.

11+ preparation from Quinton

Grammar school preparation is a common request from Quinton families. The 11+ in the West Midlands involves maths, verbal reasoning, non-verbal reasoning and, for some schools, English comprehension. A good preparation plan starts in Year 5 to allow time to identify weaknesses early, build exam stamina gradually and complete several timed practice sessions before the real test. Teaching Success structures this as a term-by-term programme rather than a last-minute revision push.
