What a Year 12 tutor should be doing

Year 12 is the first year of A-Level, and the jump from GCSE is steep: fewer subjects, far more depth, and much greater independence expected. A tutor helps a student adjust to that pace and build the study habits sixth form demands.

Where Year 12 students most often need help

The common needs are coping with the depth of AS content, weaker GCSE foundations suddenly mattering again, and building independent revision routines strong enough for A-Level. We usually support maths, the sciences and other A-Level subjects at this stage, and the free trial lesson is where we work out which subject and which specific gaps to prioritise.

The assessment that shapes Year 12

Year 12 usually ends with internal AS-style exams that shape predicted grades — the grades that drive UCAS university applications the following autumn. A strong first year matters for that reason.

How lessons work

Teaching Success matches each student to a qualified teacher for their stage and priority subject. Weekly one-hour sessions combine focused teaching, worked examples, independent practice and a short review of earlier material so progress holds. Lessons run in person in Smethwick and Birmingham, or online across the UK, from £15 an hour, and always start with a free trial so families can check the fit before committing.

Looking ahead

At the A-Level (Year 12) stage, a solid Year 12 sets up the UCAS application and the Year 13 final exams from a position of strength. We keep every plan tied to the next real milestone so lessons always have a clear purpose.
