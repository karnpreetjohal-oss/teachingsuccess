What strong Year 11 GCSE revision should really do

Year 11 revision works best when it is selective and evidence-based. The strongest students do not revise everything equally; they prioritise the topics, subjects and question habits that are making the biggest difference to their total score.

What strong Year 11 GCSE revision should include

  • Using mock papers to decide what revision matters most over the next month
  • Balancing content review with full-paper timing and judgement under pressure
  • Keeping English, maths and science revision realistic enough to maintain every week
  • Using short review cycles so one round of revision actually sticks
