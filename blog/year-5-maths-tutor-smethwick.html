What Year 5 Maths covers

Year 5 is a big step up in ambition. Children multiply and divide by 10, 100 and 1,000, learn formal long multiplication and short division, add and subtract fractions with different denominators, convert between fractions, decimals and percentages, and meet prime, square and cube numbers.

Where Year 5 students most often need help

This is the year where earlier gaps really show. Long multiplication exposes weak tables; fraction work exposes a shaky understanding of equivalence. Children who 'get by' in Year 4 can suddenly find several topics hard at once.

The assessment that shapes Year 5

Year 5 has no national test, but it is really the first half of the two-year KS2 SATs run. Schools track progress closely now, so it is the best year to close gaps before the Year 6 pressure begins in earnest.

What a weekly Year 5 Maths plan looks like

  • Formal long multiplication and short division, set out reliably
  • Adding and subtracting fractions by finding common denominators
  • Links between fractions, decimals and percentages
  • Prime, square and cube numbers and simple factor work

Looking ahead

Year 5 is really the first half of the KS2 SATs journey, so we build the habits — clear working, checking, and reasoning — that Year 6 will lean on heavily.
