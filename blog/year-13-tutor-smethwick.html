What a Year 13 tutor should be doing

Year 13 is the final A-Level year, with university places riding on the summer exams. A tutor focuses on synoptic understanding, past papers and exam technique, while supporting the UCAS and results-day planning that runs alongside.

Where Year 13 students most often need help

The typical needs are pulling two years of content together for synoptic papers, refining exam technique to hit the top bands, and managing the pressure of offers and deadlines. We usually support maths, the sciences and other A-Level subjects at this stage, and the free trial lesson is where we work out which subject and which specific gaps to prioritise.

The assessment that shapes Year 13

The summer A-Level exams determine university places, and autumn-term mocks and predicted grades shape UCAS offers. We use past papers to target the exact topics and skills that separate the grades.
