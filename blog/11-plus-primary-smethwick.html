11 Plus Tuition in Smethwick: KS2 Reading, Maths and Reasoning

11 plus tuition in Smethwick is usually most effective when it starts with strong KS2 habits rather than a rush into mock papers. Families comparing grammar-school preparation often want one plan that covers reading, maths, verbal reasoning and non-verbal reasoning without overwhelming the child. That is the gap this page is designed to address.

What strong 11+ preparation includes

Children need breadth first and speed second. Reading accuracy, vocabulary, arithmetic fluency and reasoning patterns all matter because weak KS2 foundations make 11+ practice feel harder than it needs to be.

  • Reading comprehension that develops inference, vocabulary and evidence use
  • Maths work that secures arithmetic, fractions, problem solving and method
  • Verbal and non-verbal reasoning taught explicitly rather than left to repetition
  • Short homework tasks that keep momentum without causing burnout

How Year 4 and Year 5 plans should differ

Year 4: build fluency, vocabulary and confidence with lower-pressure reasoning exposure.

Year 5: increase timed practice, sharpen methods and introduce more mixed-paper work once the foundations are stable.

When to add mock papers

Mocks are useful once the child can explain common question types and hold concentration for a full section. If mock papers arrive too early, parents usually get a distorted picture because gaps in maths or reading drag everything down. For some pupils it helps to combine 11+ work with Year 6 English tutoring or SATs preparation so the core curriculum improves alongside reasoning.
