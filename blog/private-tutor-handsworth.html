What Handsworth families most commonly look for

The demand from Handsworth and Handsworth Wood splits fairly evenly between two groups. Primary families — particularly those with children in Years 4, 5 and 6 — are often focused on 11+ preparation and grammar school entry, which requires structured preparation well in advance of the test. Secondary families most commonly seek GCSE support in English, maths and science, and there is also meaningful demand for A-Level Biology and Chemistry from students at sixth forms across north Birmingham.

11+ preparation for Handsworth families

Handsworth Wood Girls' Academy is one of several selective schools accessible to families in this area, and the broader Birmingham grammar school network — particularly the King Edward VI Foundation schools — is a common target. Preparation for Birmingham's selective schools requires working at a harder level than standard 11+ materials provide. Serena handles all primary and 11+ preparation and structures sessions to build reasoning speed, English comprehension and maths problem-solving from Year 4, with a progressive increase in difficulty as the test approaches in Year 6.

In-person and online options for Handsworth

Handsworth is close enough to the Smethwick base that in-person sessions are genuinely practical for many families — the journey is short and can be fitted around school pick-up times. Online sessions are also available and suit families with more flexible scheduling needs or older students who prefer working at home. A number of Handsworth families use a combination — in-person during term time and online during busy weeks or when travel is difficult.
