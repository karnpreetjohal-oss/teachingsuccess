Why gap-closing beats general revision

GCSE Maths has over 90 topic areas. Most students who are getting a grade 3 or 4 are not struggling across all of them — they typically have 10 to 20 weak topics that are consistently costing them marks. Identifying and closing those specific gaps produces faster grade improvements than general revision across the whole spec.

What Mr Vasta focuses on in GCSE Maths sessions

  • Reviewing recent mock paper or school assessment to identify the biggest gaps
  • Foundation tier: number, fractions, percentages, ratio, basic algebra and geometry
  • Higher tier: quadratics, simultaneous equations, circle theorems, vectors, functions
  • Method marks — always show clear working to secure partial credit
  • Non-calculator skills — a paper many students underprepare for
  • Timed past paper practice under exam conditions every few sessions

30-day progress plan

  1. Week 1: review recent test or mock, agree priority topic list.
  2. Week 2: reteach the highest-priority gaps — typically algebra and ratio.
  3. Week 3: mixed exam question practice on improved topics, find next round of gaps.
  4. Week 4: timed past paper section, measure improvement, reset priority list.
