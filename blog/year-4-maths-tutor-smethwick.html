What Year 4 Maths covers

Year 4 is dominated by one national checkpoint: the Multiplication Tables Check (MTC) in June, which tests all tables up to 12×12 against the clock. Alongside this, children learn formal short multiplication, extend place value to 10,000, work with decimals and negative numbers, and calculate area and perimeter.

Where Year 4 students most often need help

Even able children can freeze on the MTC because it is timed — recall that is 'nearly there' is not fast enough. The other frequent gap is short multiplication, where a shaky grasp of tables makes the whole method unreliable.

The assessment that shapes Year 4

The Multiplication Tables Check in June is the defining event of Year 4 — 25 questions on tables up to 12×12, each with a six-second limit. Because it is timed, we practise for speed and accuracy in short, calm bursts rather than long revision sessions.

What a weekly Year 4 Maths plan looks like

  • Timed, low-pressure practice of all tables to 12×12 for the June MTC
  • Formal short multiplication built on secure table facts
  • Place value to 10,000 and an introduction to decimals
  • Area and perimeter of rectangles, with clear working
