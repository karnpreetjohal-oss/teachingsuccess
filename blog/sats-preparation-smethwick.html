What SATs support should focus on

This page is aimed at families looking for a clearer SAT prep route across Birmingham and Smethwick. The strongest support usually combines core curriculum teaching with short timed tasks, so the child improves in class while also becoming more familiar with test format and pacing.

  • Reading comprehension and inference work for Year 6 English papers
  • Arithmetic fluency, reasoning and multi-step problem solving in maths
  • SPaG practice that improves grammar accuracy without becoming repetitive
  • Short timed sections so test confidence grows gradually

Year 2 and Year 6 need different plans

Year 2 support should stay lighter and confidence-led, with simple routines that improve reading, phonics, number facts and written accuracy. Year 6 usually needs a more explicit structure: reading speed, arithmetic fluency, question technique and a weekly pattern of practice that reduces panic closer to the tests.
