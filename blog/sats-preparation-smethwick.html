Year 2 and Year 6 need different plans

Year 2 support should stay lighter and confidence-led, with simple routines that improve reading, phonics, number facts and written accuracy. Year 6 usually needs a more explicit structure: reading speed, arithmetic fluency, question technique and a weekly pattern of practice that reduces panic closer to the tests.
