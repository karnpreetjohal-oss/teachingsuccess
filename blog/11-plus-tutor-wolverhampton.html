Mid-August 2026: turn Wolverhampton 11+ scores into a final teaching plan

For Wolverhampton pupils sitting a September entrance test, the next lesson should begin with section evidence rather than a general revision list. One weak reasoning type or English habit can be improved; a complete last-minute rebuild cannot.

  • Compare accuracy with pace: A low section score may come from unfinished questions rather than missing knowledge.
  • Revisit the error after a gap: Return to a corrected question two days later to check that the method has stuck.
  • Mix familiar and unfamiliar items: This builds confidence without making practice predictable.
  • Plan the final fortnight: Use the 11+ readiness checklist to decide what to maintain and what to stop.
