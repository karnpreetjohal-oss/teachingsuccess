What strong English tuition in Smethwick should include

Good English tuition should make the next step obvious. In primary, that often means reading fluency, vocabulary and confident writing. In KS3, it means structure, precision and stronger written explanations. At GCSE, it means building answers that actually earn marks. The lesson plan should match the stage rather than treating English as one generic subject.

  • Reading and writing assessed separately so gaps are not blurred together
  • Live feedback on sentence control, paragraphing and explanation
  • Model answers or exemplars that show what better looks like
  • Regular review so families can see progress in confidence and written quality
