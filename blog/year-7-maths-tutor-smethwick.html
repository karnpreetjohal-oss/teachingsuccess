What Year 7 Maths covers

Year 7 is the start of secondary maths, and the pace changes noticeably. Students formalise negative numbers, meet proper algebraic notation and solving equations, build fluency across fractions, decimals and percentages, and start ratio and proportion — often across a fortnightly topic cycle that moves faster than primary school.

Where Year 7 students most often need help

The common pressure point is that the new school routine makes primary gaps feel bigger. In maths this shows up as hesitation with method, weak or missing working, and losing accuracy once a question becomes multi-step.

The assessment that shapes Year 7

There is no external exam in Year 7, but most secondaries run regular assessments and set by ability early. Getting off to a confident start protects a child from being placed in a lower set that is hard to climb out of later.

What a weekly Year 7 Maths plan looks like

  • Confident work with negative numbers and order of operations
  • The move from words to algebra: notation, substitution and one-step equations
  • Secure fraction, decimal and percentage conversions
  • Introductory ratio and proportion with clear reasoning

How lessons work

Looking ahead

A solid Year 7 protects against the widening gaps that make Years 8 and 9 difficult, and it sets up the algebra that GCSE will eventually depend on.
