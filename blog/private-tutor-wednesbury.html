What Wednesbury families most commonly need

GCSE maths is the most frequent request from Wednesbury at secondary level, followed by GCSE English Language and combined science. KS3 support for Year 7 to Year 9 pupils comes up regularly — particularly for students who struggled with the Year 6 to Year 7 transition and have developed gaps in maths or reading comprehension that school has not yet addressed. At primary level, Year 5 and Year 6 families sometimes request SATs preparation, and occasionally 11+ preparation for families targeting selective schools in the wider West Midlands area.

Online sessions for Wednesbury families

For most Wednesbury families, online tuition is the most practical format. It removes travel time, allows sessions to be scheduled immediately after school, and works particularly well for secondary students who are confident with screen-based work. Kevin runs GCSE sessions online using a shared digital workspace where past paper questions, worked examples and student responses can all be displayed and annotated in real time. The session format is active and structured — not a video call where the student watches someone explain.

GCSE outcomes in Sandwell and what tuition changes

GCSE outcomes across Sandwell schools vary significantly — both between schools and within year groups. One-to-one tuition makes the most difference when it is targeted at the specific topics and question types that are losing marks in assessments, rather than broadly revisiting all content. Kevin's approach starts with a diagnostic session to identify exactly where the gaps are, then plans the first half-term of sessions around those priorities before broadening out to other areas.
