What to compare first

The best tuition setup usually includes a clear starting point, a lesson structure matched to the student’s age and subject, and regular updates so parents can see what is improving. Good tutors should be able to explain how they assess gaps, how often they review progress and what the student is expected to do between lessons.

  • Subject fit and year-group fit, not just general availability
  • Clear lesson planning rather than vague promises about improvement
  • Regular communication on progress, confidence and next steps
  • Evidence that the tutor can teach, not just supervise homework

Questions worth asking before you book

Ask how the tutor measures progress, how the lesson plan changes when a student is stuck, and whether the support is designed for the exact stage your child is in. A parent looking for 11+ help needs a different answer from one looking for a maths tutor in Smethwick, Year 7 transition support or GCSE revision.
