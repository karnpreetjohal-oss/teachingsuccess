What Year 8 maths tuition should build

Good Year 8 maths tuition should improve algebra fluency, fraction work, ratio reasoning and confidence with longer problem-solving questions. That gives students a much stronger base for Year 9 and early GCSE topics.

The algebraic bridge: what Year 8 Maths prepares for GCSE

Year 8 Maths covers the algebraic and geometric foundations that GCSE Higher builds on directly. The key topics — forming and solving equations from word problems, expanding double brackets, factorising, plotting and interpreting straight-line graphs, and the properties of 2D and 3D shapes — are not just Year 8 content. They are the content that GCSE questions are built from. Students who cannot form an equation from a word problem in Year 8 will struggle with quadratic word problems in Year 10 for the same underlying reason.
