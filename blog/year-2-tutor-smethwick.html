What a Year 2 tutor should really be helping with

Year 2 support should focus on settling into classroom routines, early reading confidence and basic number security. At this stage, the aim is usually to make school feel steadier week by week rather than to pile on extra work without a plan.

Where Year 2 pupils usually start to wobble

The common pressure point in Year 2 is when a child understands orally but still needs help recording ideas independently. A strong plan makes those problems visible early, then works through them calmly before they grow into a bigger Key Stage 2 issue.

What the first half-term of Year 2 tuition should include

  • Short checks on phonics security, early handwriting, sentence building and talking before writing so the tutor can see what is secure and what still needs direct teaching
  • Regular work on number bonds, counting, place value and simple one-step problems rather than waiting until the next school test exposes the gap again
  • A simple weekly routine that parents and pupils can actually maintain
  • Enough review to build confidence, not just enough to finish the worksheet
