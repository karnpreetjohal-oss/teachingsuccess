What Year 2 tutoring should focus on

Good Year 2 tutoring should strengthen phonics, number confidence, handwriting and simple written answers while keeping routines manageable. Small, steady gains at this stage usually make school feel much easier very quickly.

What Year 2 sessions at Teaching Success cover

  • Phonics — consolidating all Phase 5 sounds and common exception words
  • Reading fluency — decoding unfamiliar words independently and reading with expression
  • Number bonds and counting — secure recall to 20 and beyond, leading into addition and subtraction
  • Simple sentence writing — full stops, capital letters and basic punctuation applied confidently

How Serena structures Year 2 sessions

Year 2 sessions with Serena are kept to 45 to 50 minutes — long enough to make meaningful progress but short enough to match a Year 2 child's natural attention span. Each session opens with a quick warm-up (a phonics sound or a number fact game), moves into one clear teaching objective, and ends with a short guided practice task. A small home activity is set each week to keep momentum without adding pressure.

What parents should look for in the first four weeks

  1. Week 1: Your child is relaxed and engaged in the session and can explain what they worked on.
  2. Week 2: Phonics sounds or number facts from week 1 are coming back more confidently.
  3. Week 3: Written work at home or at school shows one specific improvement from tuition.
  4. Week 4: First progress update from Serena covering what has improved and what to focus on next.
