The Birmingham 11+ Format

What the grammar school test actually covers.

Birmingham grammar schools — including King Edward's — use GL Assessment format papers. The test splits into four timed sections. Our mock replicates this exactly so your child sits the real thing having already done it once.

📐

Maths

Arithmetic, problem-solving, number, shape and data — all under time pressure.

50 mins
📖

English

Reading comprehension, vocabulary, spelling and grammar questions on unseen passages.

50 mins
🔤

Verbal Reasoning

Word relationships, analogies, codes and sequences — tests language and logic together.

50 mins
🧩

Non-Verbal Reasoning

Pattern recognition, spatial awareness and visual sequences without relying on language.

45 mins
Why It Matters

What a mock test shows that practice papers don't.

Practice papers done at home have no clock pressure, no mixed sections back-to-back and no real stakes. A proper mock test reveals how your child performs when all of that is added at once.

1

Real time pressure

Many children know the content but drop marks when they have to keep pace. The mock shows whether pacing is actually a problem before it matters on the real day.

2

Stamina across all sections

Sitting four timed sections back-to-back is different from doing one practice paper. Concentration often fades in the third or fourth section — this is the only way to see it early.

3

A score you can act on

The section breakdown tells you exactly where to focus in the weeks before exam day — not a vague "keep practising" but a specific weak area and a plan.
