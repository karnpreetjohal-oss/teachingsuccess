What strong GCSE exam technique support should really do

Exam technique is what turns knowledge into marks. Many students know more than their paper score suggests, but they lose marks through timing, weak structure, incomplete method or misreading the command word.

What strong GCSE exam technique support should include

  • Understanding how mark schemes actually reward method, evidence and precision
  • Learning when to move on, when to return and how to manage paper timing
  • Practising under realistic conditions instead of only reading model answers
  • Using mock papers to spot repeated exam habits, not just repeated topic gaps

When this route is the right fit

This route is most useful when the student can often explain answers verbally but still underperforms on the actual paper.
