Read a mark scheme without mistaking it for a model essay

A mark scheme can clarify what an assessment rewards, but it may not be written as the answer a pupil should produce. Use it alongside the question and the student’s own work.

A practical example

Choose one marked response and identify where a required idea is present, absent or unclear. In a longer answer, discuss the quality of the explanation rather than counting phrases copied from the mark scheme.

Turn a mark-scheme point into an explanation in your own words
