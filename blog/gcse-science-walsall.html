Compare percentages only after checking the denominators

Scientific comparisons can be misleading when two groups have different sizes. Work with the denominator before deciding which result is larger.

A practical example

In a fictional dataset, 12 of 40 seeds germinate in one condition and 18 of 90 in another. The percentages are 30% and 20%. The second group has more germinated seeds in total, but a smaller proportion. Both statements can be true.

Write the conclusion with the quantity named: number germinated or percentage germinated. Then discuss what further information would be needed to judge the investigation, including how the groups were selected. Online tuition can review this reasoning; do not assume a separate Walsall teaching location.
