What GCSE Science sessions cover

  • Biology: cells, genetics, evolution, disease, ecology, homeostasis
  • Chemistry: atomic structure, bonding, rates of reaction, electrolysis, organic chemistry
  • Physics: forces, energy, waves, electricity, magnetism, space
  • Required practicals — method, variables, results analysis
  • 6-mark questions — how to structure a full-mark response
  • Command words: describe, explain, evaluate, compare, calculate
  • Past papers and mark scheme practice

The most common places students lose marks in Science

The three biggest sources of avoidable mark losses in GCSE Science are: not explaining the mechanism (stating the fact without explaining why), weak required practical write-ups, and poor structure on 6-mark questions. Mr Vasta specifically targets these in sessions because fixing them produces the fastest grade improvements.
