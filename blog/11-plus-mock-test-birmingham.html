FAQ

Common questions.

What format is the Birmingham 11+ mock test?

GL Assessment — the format used by King Edward's and most West Midlands grammar schools. Four sections: maths, English, verbal and non-verbal reasoning, all timed.

Where is the mock test held?

At our Smethwick base (B66), easily reached from across Birmingham, Bearwood, Oldbury, West Bromwich and Harborne. Weekend morning sessions only.

What year group is it suitable for?

Year 4, 5 or 6. Year 5 gives the most actionable result — enough time left to change the preparation plan before the real exam in September/October of Year 6.

What feedback do we receive?

A section-by-section score breakdown, comparison against the grammar school benchmark, and written teacher notes on the weakest areas and what to work on next.
