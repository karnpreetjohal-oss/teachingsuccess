Secondary School Tutor in Wolverhampton: Year 7 to Year 11 Support

Secondary school is where small gaps become big problems. A student who is slightly behind in Year 8 maths can find GCSE algebra very difficult two years later. Mr Olu is a qualified secondary school teacher offering tuition across Year 7 to Year 11 in Wolverhampton — covering the full KS3 and KS4 pathway.

Why secondary school tuition is different from primary

Secondary school content moves fast. Teachers cover a new topic every week or two, and students who fall behind in one area quickly find the next topic harder. A secondary school tutor who works in a classroom every day — as Mr Olu does — understands exactly where the curriculum accelerates and where students need reinforcement before moving forward.

Year 7, 8 & 9 — KS3 Support

KS3 is the best time to build strong habits and close gaps before they compound. Tuition at this stage focuses on:

  • Reinforcing primary maths: fractions, decimals, percentages, negative numbers
  • Introducing algebra — the topic most students find difficult in Year 7
  • Science: understanding the three disciplines before GCSE separates them
  • Study skills and homework habits that carry through to GCSE

Year 10 & 11 — GCSE Preparation

At GCSE the focus shifts to grade targets. Mr Olu works backwards from the student's target grade to identify which topics need the most work and structures sessions around closing those gaps efficiently.

  • Topic-by-topic gap analysis using recent mocks and school assessments
  • Exam board specific content — AQA, Edexcel, OCR
  • Past paper practice under timed conditions
  • 6-mark and extended answer technique
  • Final revision planning in the run-up to exams

How Parents Can Track Progress

After each block of 4 sessions, parents receive a brief progress note covering: topics covered, areas of improvement, current gaps still to address, and the focus for the next block. There are no surprises — progress is transparent throughout.
