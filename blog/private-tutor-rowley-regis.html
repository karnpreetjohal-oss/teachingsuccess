Private Tutor in Rowley Regis

Rowley Regis (B65) is a Sandwell area in the south-west of the borough, sitting between Oldbury and Cradley Heath. It is slightly further from the Teaching Success base in Smethwick than areas like Oldbury or West Bromwich, which makes online sessions the most practical option for most Rowley Regis families — though in-person sessions can be arranged depending on location and timing.

Typical tuition needs in Rowley Regis

Demand from Rowley Regis families follows the same pattern as the wider Sandwell area: GCSE support at secondary level, KS2 catch-up and SATs preparation at primary, and occasional KS3 intervention for Year 7 and Year 8 students. GCSE maths, English Language and science are the three subjects that come up most frequently. At primary level, Year 5 and Year 6 families often seek SATs support, and there is some demand for Year 4 and Year 5 maths and English to get ahead of the SATs year with a stronger foundation.

How online sessions work for Rowley Regis students

Kevin and Serena run all online sessions via video call with a shared workspace, so students can see worked examples, complete exam questions and have their working reviewed in real time. For GCSE students, this means past paper practice from the correct exam board with immediate feedback on method and mark allocation. For primary students, Serena adapts the format to be more active and varied — shorter tasks with regular changes of activity to maintain focus across the full session hour.

Starting tuition from Rowley Regis

The free trial lesson is the most straightforward starting point. It runs as a full teaching session — not a sales call — and gives both the student and the family a clear picture of what regular sessions would look like. Kevin or Serena will assess the student's current level during the trial and explain what the first half-term of sessions would focus on before any commitment is made.
