Planning from West Bromwich

Check the application route before building a school list.

A nearby school is not automatically a grammar school, and a practice-paper result does not establish eligibility. Start with each school’s published policy.

Grammar-school test and application

Confirm test registration through the official arrangements and also complete your home local authority’s preference form. The Birmingham grammar-school list includes Five Ways, Aston and both King Edward VI Handsworth schools.

Check eligibility and the journey

Read the entry-year policy before relying on distance or a score. The Five Ways admissions page links its policies and timetable. Check the real school-day journey rather than assuming a fixed driving time from West Bromwich.

Other admissions routes

Do not treat Q3 Academy Langley as a shared 11+ test destination; its admissions page directs applications through the local authority. King Edward’s School is an independent school with its own process.

Session Structure

What a typical session looks like.

Sessions are one hour, one-to-one, with a consistent structure that builds habits over time — not just covers material once.

1

Warm-up (10 min)

Quick-fire mental arithmetic for maths sessions; vocabulary recall for English sessions. The goal is automatic recall, not effortful calculation or word-search. Speed drills become a reliable habit over several months.

2

Topic work (20 min)

Focused teaching or review of the week's topic area — fractions, inference, verbal codes, non-verbal patterns. Explanation, worked examples, and guided practice using real GL Assessment question formats.

3

Timed practice (20 min)

A section from a full past paper, completed under timed conditions. Immediate marking and walkthrough of every error — not "this was wrong, the answer was X" but "here is the thinking that led to the right answer and where yours went wrong."

Practical guide · Updated 23 September 2026

Use one diagram for a multi-step ratio story

A bar model can help a pupil keep track of the whole in a word problem. The drawing must represent the actual relationship rather than become a decoration beside the calculation.

A practical example

In an original practice story, red and blue counters are in the ratio 2:3 and there are 30 counters altogether. Draw five equal parts: each represents six counters, giving twelve red and eighteen blue. Label the total across all five parts.

Change the question so that the eighteen blue counters are given instead of the total. Explain why the same diagram still works. Confirm the learner’s starting point before adding timed practice, and discuss available online or Smethwick-based lessons with the current team.

Check whether a ratio problem gives a total or a difference

In an original practice question, red and blue counters are in the ratio 2:3 with 25 counters altogether. Five parts make 25, so the groups contain 10 and 15. Draw the five equal parts before calculating.

Now say there are five more blue counters than red. The difference is one part, again giving 10 and 15. Although these examples share an answer, the given quantities represent different things. Ask the child to label the known information so the method is explained rather than memorised.

How long does 11+ preparation take from West Bromwich?

Children starting in Year 4 or 5 have 12–18 months — the most effective approach. Year 6 starters can still make significant progress in 6–9 months with two sessions per week and consistent home practice. We tailor the intensity to the time available.
