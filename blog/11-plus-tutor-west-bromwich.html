Grammar School Routes from West Bromwich

Which grammar schools West Bromwich families apply for.

West Bromwich sits in Sandwell borough — outside Birmingham but with access to the full range of Birmingham grammar schools via the shared GL Assessment process. Here are the most common targets for families in this area.

King Edward's Five Ways (Mixed)

The most geographically accessible King Edward's school for West Bromwich families. Located in Bartley Green, just south of the A456. Academically strong and well-regarded. Regularly the first-choice King Edward's school for children in the Sandwell corridor.

Handsworth Grammar School (Boys)

A boys' grammar independent of the KE foundation. Located in Handsworth — straightforward to reach from West Bromwich via the A41. Popular with Sandwell families as a realistic stretch target. Consistently strong academic outcomes.

King Edward's Handsworth (Girls)

Girls' grammar in Handsworth Wood. Many West Bromwich girls include this alongside King Edward's Five Ways on their school list. Competitive entry but achievable with systematic preparation. Strong tradition of academic and extracurricular achievement.

Q3 Academy Langley (Boys)

Selective boys' school in Oldbury — very close to West Bromwich. Uses the 11+ selective process and is an achievable target for boys who may not quite reach the King Edward's benchmark. A strong school with close community links in Sandwell.

King Edward's Aston (Boys)

Boys' grammar in Aston with a strong STEM tradition. Accessible from West Bromwich via public transport or car. Boys who perform well in preparation are encouraged to include this on their list alongside other schools.

King Edward's Boys (Edgbaston)

The most selective grammar school in the region — boys performing in the top 3–5% nationally. West Bromwich boys with strong potential are prepared for this as part of a balanced school list. Teaching Success is clear about benchmarks from the start so expectations are realistic.

The Test

What the GL Assessment 11+ covers.

All Birmingham grammar schools use the same GL Assessment format. Four timed sections, all multiple-choice. Teaching Success covers every section.

Maths · 50 min Arithmetic, fractions, shape and data

Kevin covers mental arithmetic speed, fractions and percentages, multi-step problems, shape and data questions. All in GL Assessment multiple-choice format from day one.

English · 50 min Comprehension, vocabulary, cloze

Serena covers inference strategies, systematic vocabulary building, synonym and antonym practice, cloze passages and grammar — the full English paper in one programme.

VR & NVR · 95 min total Verbal and non-verbal reasoning

Verbal reasoning combines language and logic — covered by Serena alongside English. Non-verbal reasoning uses visual patterns — covered by Kevin alongside maths. Both improve rapidly with regular timed practice.

Session Structure

What a typical session looks like.

Sessions are one hour, one-to-one, with a consistent structure that builds habits over time — not just covers material once.

1

Warm-up (10 min)

Quick-fire mental arithmetic for maths sessions; vocabulary recall for English sessions. The goal is automatic recall, not effortful calculation or word-search. Speed drills become a reliable habit over several months.

2

Topic work (20 min)

Focused teaching or review of the week's topic area — fractions, inference, verbal codes, non-verbal patterns. Explanation, worked examples, and guided practice using real GL Assessment question formats.

3

Timed practice (20 min)

A section from a full past paper, completed under timed conditions. Immediate marking and walkthrough of every error — not "this was wrong, the answer was X" but "here is the thinking that led to the right answer and where yours went wrong."

Which grammar schools are accessible from West Bromwich?

Most commonly: King Edward's Five Ways, Handsworth Grammar (Boys), King Edward's Handsworth (Girls), Q3 Academy Langley and King Edward's Aston. All use GL Assessment format and are reachable from West Bromwich within 15–20 minutes.

Is online or in-person tuition better for West Bromwich children?

Both work well. In-person sessions at Smethwick are roughly 4–5 miles away. Online sessions use identical materials and include timed practice papers sent in advance. The choice depends on your family's schedule and whether your child works better with direct teacher presence.

How long does 11+ preparation take from West Bromwich?

Children starting in Year 4 or 5 have 12–18 months — the most effective approach. Year 6 starters can still make significant progress in 6–9 months with two sessions per week and consistent home practice. We tailor the intensity to the time available.
