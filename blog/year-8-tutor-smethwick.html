What a Year 8 tutor should be doing

Year 8 is a consolidation year that quietly matters: algebra deepens, English analysis develops, and science becomes more demanding. A tutor keeps a student progressing in a year that is easy to coast through.

Where Year 8 students most often need help

By Year 8, small unaddressed weaknesses compound — sign errors in algebra, analysis that identifies but does not explain, and gaps that will surface at GCSE. We usually support maths, English and science at this stage, and the free trial lesson is where we work out which subject and which specific gaps to prioritise.

The assessment that shapes Year 8

Year 8 has no national exam, but assessments feed into GCSE tier and set decisions in many schools. Targeted support now prevents a harder Year 9 and beyond.
