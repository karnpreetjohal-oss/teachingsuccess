What Year 6 English tutoring should focus on

A strong plan needs to combine core KS2 English teaching with SATs familiarity. That means better reading accuracy, more confident inference, clearer sentence control and regular writing practice, not just worksheet repetition or last-minute paper drilling.

  • Reading comprehension with evidence-based answers and stronger inference
  • SPaG work on punctuation, clause control, grammar accuracy and editing
  • Writing practice that improves structure, vocabulary and sentence variety
  • Short SATs-style tasks so test format becomes more familiar each week

A useful 4-week English plan

  1. Week 1: reading baseline, vocabulary checks and answer structure.
  2. Week 2: grammar focus with sentence editing and punctuation drills.
  3. Week 3: writing task with feedback on structure and accuracy.
  4. Week 4: mixed SATs-style practice with a clear progress summary.

When to start before SATs

Ideally, support should begin early enough to build habits rather than relying on a short burst of revision. Children usually make better progress when tutoring improves their day-to-day English confidence as well as their SATs performance, because the gains start showing up in school work too.
