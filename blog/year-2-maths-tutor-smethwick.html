What Year 2 Maths covers

Year 2 is where the number sense built in Reception and Year 1 has to become reliable. Children work on place value to 100, addition and subtraction facts within 20, the 2, 5 and 10 times tables, simple fractions such as a half, a third and a quarter, money, and telling the time to five minutes.

Where Year 2 students most often need help

The usual sticking points are number bonds that are not yet automatic, counting in twos, fives and tens, and reading a word problem to decide whether to add or subtract. If recall is slow here, every later topic feels harder than it should.

The assessment that shapes Year 2

Year 2 used to end with statutory SATs; these are now optional, but many schools still use SATs-style papers internally. We can prepare children for that format so the tasks feel familiar, without turning Year 2 into a pressured exam year.

What a weekly Year 2 Maths plan looks like

  • Rapid recall of number bonds and the 2, 5 and 10 times tables through short, playful practice
  • Column-free mental methods for addition and subtraction within 100
  • Reading simple word problems and choosing the right operation
  • Recognising halves, thirds and quarters of shapes and small amounts

How lessons work

Every student starts with a free trial lesson so we can see exactly where the Key Stage 1 gaps are, using recent schoolwork or a short diagnostic. From there, weekly one-hour sessions mix direct teaching on the current priority with worked examples, independent practice and a short review of earlier topics so nothing slips. Lessons are taught by qualified teachers in Smethwick and Birmingham, or online across the UK, from £15 an hour.

Looking ahead

A confident Year 2 makes the move into formal written methods in Year 3 far smoother. Where families want it, we also prepare for the (now non-statutory) Year 2 SATs-style checks so the assessments feel familiar rather than stressful.
