What Year 4 11+ preparation should really do

Year 4 should not feel like a full exam year. The most useful work is building calm foundations: reading accuracy, number confidence, vocabulary and early reasoning habits.

What Year 4 11+ preparation should include

  • Light-touch reasoning practice so pupils learn the patterns without pressure
  • Reading and vocabulary routines that support both school English and later 11+ work
  • Maths fluency checks before harder multi-step reasoning appears
  • A measured plan that avoids burning out a child before Year 5 preparation begins

Mid-August 2026: build an incoming Year 5 foundation, not a test sprint

Children moving from Year 4 into Year 5 have time to prepare thoughtfully. This is the moment to strengthen reading range, number fluency and reasoning language before timed 11+ work becomes more prominent later in the school year.

  • Check reading stamina: Use one age-appropriate passage and discuss meaning, inference and unfamiliar words.
  • Secure number facts: Times tables and mental arithmetic should become dependable before speed is pushed.
  • Introduce reasoning language: Teach terms such as opposite, sequence, rotate and reflect through short examples.
  • Set a sustainable routine: Use the 11+ readiness checklist as a starting benchmark, not a pass-or-fail test.
