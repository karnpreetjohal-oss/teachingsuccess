Year 6 11+ support has to become sharper and calmer at the same time. The focus is no longer broad exploration; it is timed performance, mock feedback and the exact weaknesses still costing marks.

Mid-July 2026 update for Year 6 11+ preparation

Mid-July 2026 is a practical 11+ checkpoint for Year 6 11+ preparation: West Midlands Grammar Schools registration for September 2027 entry has now closed, so families should use the waiting period before allocated test-centre information arrives in September to balance GL-style paper practice, section repair and confidence. The strongest holiday plan uses feedback carefully without overtesting the child.

  • Balance English comprehension, verbal reasoning, maths and non-verbal or spatial reasoning so one confident area does not hide a weaker one.
  • Use a small number of timed tasks to practise pace, then spend more time reviewing the mistakes.
  • Check answer-sheet habits and section timing as well as subject knowledge.
  • Keep the plan light enough to protect the holiday feel, but specific enough that September does not become a cold restart.
