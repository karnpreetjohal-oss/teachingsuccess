What one-to-one tuition should really do

One-to-one teaching is most useful when the pace, explanation and feedback need to follow the individual student rather than the shape of a group or a standard workbook.

What one-to-one tuition should include

  • Immediate correction of misunderstandings instead of waiting for them to settle into habit
  • A lesson pace that can slow down for reteaching or speed up when the student is ready
  • Better visibility for parents around what is actually improving week to week
  • Clearer links between school work, tutor work and the next target

When this route is the right fit

This route suits students who need a more personal pace, more confidence rebuilding, or more precise subject diagnosis than group settings usually allow.

Mid-August 2026: decide whether one-to-one tuition is solving the right problem

Individual tuition is most valuable when a pupil needs a personalised pace, detailed feedback or a subject route that a group cannot provide. A trial should test that need directly rather than assuming one-to-one is automatically the best format.

  • Define the reason: Name the gap, confidence issue or exam goal that needs individual attention.
  • Watch the balance: The pupil should think and explain; the tutor should not fill every silence.
  • Ask how prompts will reduce: Support should build independent starts and self-correction over time.
  • Compare after four sessions: Use schoolwork and a repeated task to judge whether the extra personalisation is working.
