The 2026 UCAT at a glance

The standard test lasts just under two hours. Verbal Reasoning, Decision Making and Quantitative Reasoning contribute to a cognitive total from 900 to 2700. Situational Judgement is reported separately as Band 1 to Band 4. There is no negative marking.

  • Verbal Reasoning: 44 questions in 22 minutes, testing careful evaluation of written information.
  • Decision Making: 35 questions in 37 minutes, covering logic, arguments, probability and complex information.
  • Quantitative Reasoning: 36 questions in 26 minutes, using tables, charts and numerical problem solving.
  • Situational Judgement: 69 questions in 26 minutes, assessing judgement in clinical and educational scenarios.

Use the official UCAT resources first

Paid tutoring is optional. The UCAT Consortium provides the only official question tutorials, question banks and practice tests, free of charge. Our role is to help students use those materials deliberately through diagnosis, strategy feedback, mock review and an achievable weekly plan.

  • Official preparation guidance: Start with the tour and question tutorials.
  • Official practice tests: Use these nearer the test date under realistic timed conditions.
  • UCAT universities: Check the current course and admissions-test requirements before booking.

4 September 2026: Turn the Remaining UCAT Window Into a Dated Plan

The official 2026 deadlines are now close: access-arrangement applications close at 3pm on 10 September, booking closes at 3pm on 16 September and testing ends on 24 September. Candidates should use the UCAT Consortium dates, not an old screenshot or last year's calendar.

  • Not booked: Complete registration and booking now, allowing time to solve account, payment or access-arrangement problems before the deadline.
  • More than seven days away: Use one official timed test to identify the section and decision type costing the most marks.
  • Within seven days: Place the last demanding mock early, review it fully and move toward shorter mixed sets and normal sleep.
  • Already tested: Record the result accurately, then shift attention to course selection, the 15 October UCAS deadline and each university's current policy.

The useful question is no longer “How much UCAT practice can I fit in?” It is “Which decision can still improve before my booked date without exhausting me?”

A seven-day UCAT review cycle

A mock score becomes useful only after the decisions behind it have been examined. Repeat this cycle with the time remaining, shortening the sets rather than skipping the review.

  • Day 1: Complete an official timed test or the most relevant timed subtest.
  • Day 2: Review every incorrect, guessed and unanswered item without starting another mock.
  • Days 3–4: Practise the two repeated error types first untimed, then under a short time limit.
  • Day 5: Use a mixed timed set to test whether the new decision works when question types change.
  • Days 6–7: Record the result, choose the next priority and include one lighter recovery day.

UCAT support alongside the medicine application

A strong plan keeps the admissions test connected to the wider application without pretending one score guarantees an interview. Students should check entry requirements, contextual criteria and how each university uses UCAT, then protect time for the UCAS application and interview preparation.

UCAT preparation FAQs

What sections are in the UCAT in 2026?

The four separately timed sections are Verbal Reasoning, Decision Making, Quantitative Reasoning and Situational Judgement. Abstract Reasoning is no longer part of the test.

What UCAT score should a student aim for?

There is no universal safe score. Medical and dental schools use UCAT differently, and thresholds can change between application cycles. Build an application list from each course's current selection policy.

Do students need paid UCAT tutoring or question banks?

No. The UCAT Consortium provides official tutorials, question banks and practice tests free of charge. Teaching Success tutoring is optional and adds diagnosis, planning, strategy feedback, timed-practice review and accountability.

What should a student bring to a UCAT trial session?

Bring the booked test date, recent official practice results by section and a short note of the questions or timing decisions causing the most difficulty.
