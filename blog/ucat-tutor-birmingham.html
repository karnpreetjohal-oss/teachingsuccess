What strong UCAT preparation should really do

UCAT preparation is different from school revision because the core challenge is speed, decision-making and recovery under pressure, not content memorisation.

What strong UCAT preparation should include

  • Breaking the test into sections so practice is targeted rather than repetitive
  • Tracking accuracy and speed separately, because students often need different fixes for each
  • Using short, high-pressure timed sets rather than only full mocks
  • Connecting UCAT planning to Medicine or Dentistry application goals rather than treating the test in isolation

When this route is the right fit

This route is best for students applying to Medicine or Dentistry who want a structured, section-by-section build rather than self-study alone.
