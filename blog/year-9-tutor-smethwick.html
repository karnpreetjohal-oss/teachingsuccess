What a Year 9 tutor should be doing

Year 9 is a pivot year — many Smethwick and Birmingham secondaries begin GCSE content now, and option and tier decisions are made. A tutor helps a student strengthen foundations exactly when they start to count.

Where Year 9 students most often need help

The typical needs are KS3 gaps rolling straight into GCSE content: shaky algebra, weak essay structure, and science that assumes secure earlier knowledge. We usually support maths, English and science at this stage, and the free trial lesson is where we work out which subject and which specific gaps to prioritise.

The assessment that shapes Year 9

Many schools make GCSE tier and set decisions in Year 9, and early GCSE assessments begin. Strengthening foundations now can influence Higher or Foundation entry and predicted grades.
