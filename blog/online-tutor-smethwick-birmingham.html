What strong online tuition should really do

Online tuition works best when it is chosen for the right reasons: less travel, easier scheduling, stronger consistency and a pupil who can engage well through a screen. It is not automatically better or worse than in-person; it is a different route.

What strong online tuition should include

  • Consistency across busy weeks when travel would otherwise break the routine
  • Particular value for older students, exam classes and families slightly further from the Smethwick base
  • Live annotation, worked examples and paper review without losing lesson pace
  • The option to switch between formats when the year gets busier

When this route is the right fit

This route is strongest when the student is old enough to work independently on screen and the family wants to remove unnecessary travel friction.
