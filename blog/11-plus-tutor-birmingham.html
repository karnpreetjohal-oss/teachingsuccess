School admissions routes

Separate grammar-school applications from independent-school entry.

Check the intended year of entry before choosing preparation materials. A shared entrance test does not mean every school has the same admissions policy.

Birmingham grammar schools

The council lists eight: Bishop Vesey’s; King Edward VI Aston; Camp Hill Boys; Camp Hill Girls; Five Ways; Handsworth Grammar School for Boys; Handsworth School for Girls; and Sutton Coldfield Grammar School for Girls.

Use the council’s grammar-school guidance and each school’s policy. Test registration and the local-authority preference form are separate steps.

Independent schools

King Edward’s School and King Edward’s High School for Girls have their own admissions processes. They should not be treated as entries on the shared grammar-school test list.

Check the actual policy

Read eligibility, catchment and oversubscription rules, not just a practice score. Start with the intended entry year on the Five Ways admissions page, for example. Do not use an unofficial percentage as a predicted offer.

The Test Format

What the Birmingham 11+ actually covers.

All Birmingham grammar schools use GL Assessment papers. There are four timed sections, all multiple-choice. Teaching Success covers every section with a subject-specialist teacher.

Maths Arithmetic, shape, data and problem-solving

Multi-step problems, fractions, decimals, percentages, ratio, shape and measurement. Kevin covers this as a qualified secondary maths teacher. Fast mental arithmetic is essential.

English Comprehension, vocabulary and grammar

Unseen passage with inference, synonym, cloze and grammar questions. Serena covers this as a qualified primary teacher with specialist English knowledge.

VR & NVR Verbal and non-verbal reasoning

Verbal reasoning tests language and logic together — codes, word relationships, analogies. Non-verbal reasoning uses pattern sequences without language. Both require systematic practice to improve rapidly.

Practical guide · Updated 23 September 2026

Familiarity should reduce surprises, not replace understanding

Knowing how instructions and answer sheets work can help a child concentrate on the questions. It is different from repeatedly rehearsing one commercial paper until the answers are remembered.

A practical example

Use a small selection of the current official familiarisation material. Ask the child to explain how an answer is recorded and what happens at a section change. Then practise the underlying skill with a different, appropriately pitched question.

Confirm the child’s intended entry year before using dates or instructions. Name the entry year before planning preparation

Write the intended secondary-school entry year at the top of the plan, alongside the child’s current school year. Then check the corresponding official test and admissions information rather than reusing a sibling’s old schedule.

FAQ

Common questions from Birmingham families.

Which grammar schools do Birmingham children apply to?

See the eight Birmingham grammar schools listed in the council’s guidance. Independent schools such as King Edward’s School and King Edward’s High School for Girls have separate admissions arrangements. Always check the relevant entry year.

Do you teach in-person or online?

Both. In-person sessions at our Smethwick base, accessible from across west Birmingham and Sandwell. Online sessions available for families across Birmingham and beyond — same curriculum and resources as in-person.

What does the Birmingham 11+ test cover?

Two GL Assessment papers contain separately timed sections covering English comprehension, verbal reasoning, mathematics and non-verbal/spatial reasoning. Each paper lasts approximately 50-60 minutes including instructions and examples. Follow the official test-centre information for your arrangements.

How early should Birmingham families start 11+ preparation?

Year 4 or Year 5 is ideal. Year 5 is the most productive year — enough time to cover all four subject areas and do substantial timed practice before the Year 6 exam. Starting in Year 6 is possible but requires a more intensive programme.
