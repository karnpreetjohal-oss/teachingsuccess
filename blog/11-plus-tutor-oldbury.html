The GL Assessment

What the 11+ test covers — and how we prepare for it.

All Birmingham grammar schools use GL Assessment format papers. The test has four sections. Teaching Success covers every section — with subject specialists — in a single joined-up programme.

1

Maths — 50 mins

Arithmetic, fractions, ratio, shape and data. Kevin (qualified secondary maths teacher) covers this with speed and accuracy as the focus.

2

English — 50 mins

Unseen passage with inference, vocabulary and cloze questions. Serena covers this with targeted vocabulary work and question-type strategies.

3

Verbal Reasoning — 50 mins

Word codes, analogies, letter sequences and logic. Serena covers this alongside English — the skills overlap closely.

4

Non-Verbal Reasoning — 45 mins

Pattern sequences, shape relationships and spatial questions — no language dependency. Kevin covers this alongside maths.

5

Mock test — £5

Full four-section timed sitting at our Smethwick centre. Auto-marked, section breakdown, written feedback. Weekend slots available.

6

Free trial lesson

One hour, in person or online. We assess your child and give an honest recommendation. No obligation.

Why In-Person Helps

Why in-person sessions have an edge for 11+ preparation.

Online works well — but for 11+ preparation specifically, in-person sessions have advantages that are difficult to replicate on screen.

👁

See exactly how they work

Misreading questions, skipping options, freezing under pressure — these habits are invisible on a video call. In person, a teacher spots and corrects them in the session.

⏱

Real timed conditions

Sitting a timed paper at a desk with a teacher is much closer to exam day than practising at the kitchen table. It removes the unfamiliar environment as a variable.

🔁

Immediate error correction

After each section, Kevin or Serena reviews wrong answers face-to-face. The child explains their thinking — the teacher corrects it at the root, not via a mark scheme.

FAQ

Common questions from Oldbury families.

Which grammar schools do Oldbury families apply to?

Most commonly: King Edward's Five Ways, Handsworth Grammar (Boys), King Edward's Handsworth (Girls) and King Edward's Aston. Some families also apply to King Edward's Boys or Girls in Edgbaston and Lordswood Girls' School in Harborne.

How far is Teaching Success from Oldbury?

Our Smethwick centre is approximately 2–3 miles from Oldbury town centre — typically a 10-minute drive. Online sessions are also available for families who prefer not to travel.

What is the GL Assessment and why does it matter?

GL Assessment publishes the standardised papers used by all Birmingham and Sandwell grammar schools — maths, English, verbal and non-verbal reasoning. Teaching Success uses past GL Assessment papers in every session so children practise the real format from day one.

Why is in-person tuition beneficial for 11+ prep?

In-person sessions let a teacher observe exactly how a child works — spotting habits like skipping questions, misreading instructions or stalling under pressure. These patterns are hard to spot online and impossible for parents to catch in home practice. They can be corrected quickly when caught early.
