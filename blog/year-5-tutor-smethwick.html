What Year 5 tutoring should focus on

Strong Year 5 tutoring should improve comprehension, written accuracy, arithmetic fluency and problem solving while keeping lessons calm and structured. That gives children a much better platform for Year 6, SATs and optional 11+ work.

What Year 5 sessions at Teaching Success cover

  • Fractions, decimals and percentages — the most heavily tested area in Year 6 SATs Maths Reasoning papers
  • Long multiplication and long division — building written methods to the speed and accuracy SATs arithmetic demands
  • Reading comprehension — inference, language effect and retrieval across fiction and non-fiction texts
  • Extended writing — structuring multi-paragraph responses in different genres with accurate punctuation and vocabulary

Year 5 and the 11+: what to consider

For families in Smethwick and the wider Birmingham area who are considering grammar school entry, Year 5 is the right time to start 11+ preparation. The King Edward's Foundation entrance tests and GL Assessment tests used by other local schools sit in September or October of Year 6 — meaning the preparation window is Year 5 and the first term of Year 6. Starting in Year 5 gives enough time to build verbal reasoning and non-verbal reasoning fluency gradually alongside the regular English and maths curriculum, rather than cramming both in a short window before the test.
