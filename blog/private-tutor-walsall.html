GCSE Maths, Science and English in Walsall

Walsall secondary schools — including Walsall Academy and Streetly Academy — see the same GCSE pressures as schools across the country: a demanding 9–1 grade system, content-heavy specifications and significant pressure on core grades in English and Maths. Mr Vasta focuses sessions on the areas that move the needle most efficiently: resolving specific misconceptions rather than re-teaching entire topics.

  • Maths: algebra, ratio, percentage change, quadratics, Pythagoras and trigonometry
  • Biology: cell structure, genetics, ecology, and required practicals
  • Chemistry: bonding, calculations (moles, concentrations), reaction types
  • Physics: forces, electricity, waves, and the mathematical elements that trips students up
  • English: AQA Language Paper 1 and 2 technique, Literature essay structure
