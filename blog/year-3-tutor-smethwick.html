What Year 3 tutoring should focus on

Strong Year 3 tutoring should improve reading understanding, spelling accuracy, number fluency and confidence with written methods. That balanced support helps children settle into KS2 without small gaps growing wider.

What Year 3 sessions at Teaching Success cover

  • Times tables — building from the 2s, 5s and 10s into the 3s, 4s and 8s using spaced practice
  • Written addition and subtraction — moving from mental methods to column methods accurately
  • Reading comprehension — retrieving specific information and making simple inferences
  • Spelling and sentence writing — prefixes, suffixes, common exception words and accurate punctuation

Why Year 3 is a critical year for intervention

Year 3 is where the curriculum jumps significantly from Year 2. Children move from informal mental methods to formal written procedures, from phonics-based reading to comprehension of longer texts, and from supported sentence writing to independent paragraphs. A gap in any of these areas that was manageable in Year 2 can grow quickly in Year 3 without support. Teaching Success sessions identify which of these transitions is causing the most difficulty and address it directly before the end of the school year.
