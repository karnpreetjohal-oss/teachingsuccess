What Year 10 maths tuition should build

Good Year 10 maths tuition should improve method accuracy, calculator confidence, algebra fluency and performance on longer reasoning questions. That gives students a much firmer base for the intensity of Year 11.

Year 10 GCSE Maths: what the specification covers

Most GCSE Maths Year 10 courses work through the highest-weighted topic areas: algebraic methods (solving quadratics, simultaneous equations, inequalities), geometry and trigonometry (Pythagoras, Sine Rule, circle theorems), and data handling (cumulative frequency, histograms). These are the topics most heavily tested in Paper 2 and Paper 3. Sessions track the school's scheme of work so that tuition and classroom teaching reinforce each other rather than running in parallel.

Using mock results to reshape the Year 10 plan

  • Year 10 internal assessments and mock papers are the single most useful diagnostic tool — Kevin reviews them at the start of a new session block
  • Topics that score less than 50% on a mock become the next priority regardless of what is currently being taught in school
  • Calculator and non-calculator performance are assessed separately — many students have significant gaps on one paper that don't show on the other
  • A written progress summary is provided every four weeks so families can track which topics have closed
