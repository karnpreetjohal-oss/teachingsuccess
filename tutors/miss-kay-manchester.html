Why Families Book

What Manchester Parents Usually Need Help With

Most GCSE Science enquiries come from students who understand the content in lessons but can't seem to convert that understanding into exam marks. That gap — between knowing something and being able to write a correct 6-mark answer — is exactly where qualified teaching experience makes a difference.

Miss Kay's sessions focus on closing that gap. She identifies which topics are genuinely understood versus which ones only feel familiar, and she teaches the specific exam writing skills — command word responses, structured explanations, data analysis — that the mark scheme actually rewards.

For primary families, the need is usually earlier intervention: building number fluency, reading comprehension and writing confidence before secondary school expectations land.

What To Expect

How Lessons With Miss Kay Work

Every student starts with a free trial lesson so Miss Kay can see the real picture — what they know, where they stall, and what the exam board expects from them. That session informs the plan for all subsequent lessons.

For GCSE Science students, sessions typically combine retrieval of prior knowledge, reteaching of the tricky conceptual areas, then immediate application through exam-style questions. Miss Kay has marked enough papers to know exactly how answers are scored, and she trains students to write answers the way examiners mark them.

For primary students, sessions focus on building solid foundations in number or literacy — whichever is the priority — with enough structured practice to make the improvement visible within a few weeks.
