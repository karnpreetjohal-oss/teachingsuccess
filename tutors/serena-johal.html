About Serena

A Qualified Primary Teacher Who Makes Reading and Writing Click

Serena Johal is a qualified primary school teacher and co-founder of Teaching Success. She works with children from Reception through to Year 6, with particular depth in KS2 English, SATs preparation and 11+ tuition. Her approach is calm, structured and focused on building confidence alongside accuracy — the two things primary-age children need most before exam pressure arrives.

Serena understands the gap between what children can do in a relaxed classroom setting and what they produce under timed test conditions. Her sessions are designed to close that gap — improving reading speed and comprehension, strengthening written expression, and building the SPaG accuracy that Year 6 SATs and 11+ papers demand.

She works in-person from the Smethwick base for families nearby and online for families who prefer the flexibility. Primary-age children often do better in familiar surroundings, so Serena is happy to advise on format during the free trial session.
