Questions

Common QTS and mentoring questions.

Do you help people pass QTS?

We provide preparation, mentoring and evidence support. QTS itself must come through an approved training or assessment route.

What teaching resources are included?

You can access practical resources such as lesson plan templates, starters, behaviour scripts and observation checklists.

Can you help with lesson planning?

Yes. We can help with lesson structure, timings, differentiation, questioning and marking routines.

Can I book one session?

Yes. Book a one-off review, a short block before an observation or regular mentoring.
