Six-week Year 6 SATs revision planner

A balanced plan for Maths arithmetic and reasoning, English reading, grammar, punctuation and spelling. Each week uses short sessions, one clear priority and enough rest to keep confidence intact.

LengthSix weeks
Weekly timeThree or four sessions of 20-30 minutes
SubjectsReading, GPS and Maths

Set a useful baseline

Before week one, use a recent school assessment or one age-appropriate practice paper to identify the three areas that lose the most marks. Do not begin by completing paper after paper. A baseline is useful only when it changes what is taught next.

Keep the workload proportionate. Year 6 pupils still need normal reading, sleep, movement and family time. Short revision that responds to actual errors is more useful than a crowded timetable.

The six-week plan

WeekMaths focusEnglish focusEnd-of-week check
Week 1
Accuracy first		Revisit written and mental methods for the four operations. Complete two short arithmetic sets without a timer.Read one short text closely. Practise retrieval questions and underline the exact evidence used.Can the child explain two corrected errors without a prompt?
Week 2
Fractions and inference		Fractions, decimals and percentages: equivalence, ordering and finding fractions or percentages of amounts.Inference: explain what a character thinks or feels and support the answer with words from the text.Repeat five questions from earlier in the week and compare accuracy.
Week 3
Reasoning and GPS		Two-step word problems, units, ratio and deciding which operation is needed before calculating.Sentence structure, word classes, punctuation choices and a focused spelling pattern.Sort errors into method, reading, arithmetic or carelessness.
Week 4
Shape, data and vocabulary		Area, perimeter, angles, coordinates, tables, graphs and reading scales accurately.Meaning in context, synonyms, summary and explaining how a word or phrase affects the reader.Complete one mixed 25-minute session, then correct it without looking at the answers first.
Week 5
Timed sections		One arithmetic section and one reasoning section on separate days. Practise moving on and returning.One reading section and one short GPS set. Keep a list of recurring spelling and punctuation errors.Check pacing: were easy marks lost because too long was spent on one question?
Week 6
Consolidate		Revisit the three Maths topics that still produce errors. Avoid introducing a large new topic.Revisit two reading question types and the highest-frequency GPS errors. Include normal reading for enjoyment.Finish with a short confidence review: what is now easier, and what is the plan if a question feels difficult?

A repeatable weekly rhythm

Use the same simple pattern each week. Predictability reduces negotiation and makes it easier to notice progress.

SessionLengthStructure
Session 120-25 minutesTeach or review one method, then complete five carefully chosen questions.
Session 220-25 minutesApply the same skill in a different context and correct mistakes immediately.
Session 320-30 minutesMix the skill with earlier learning so the child must choose the method independently.
Optional session 415-20 minutesRetrieve spellings, arithmetic facts or vocabulary. Keep this session light.

What the statutory tests cover

The current Key Stage 2 test structure includes English grammar, punctuation and spelling; English reading; Mathematics Paper 1 arithmetic; and Mathematics Papers 2 and 3 reasoning. Writing is judged through teacher assessment rather than a separate externally marked SATs paper.
