11 Plus readiness checklist

A calm, practical way to see which skills are secure and which ones need teaching. Tick an item only when your child can complete it independently, explain their method and repeat it under sensible time pressure.

Best forYears 4 and 5
Review time20 minutes with a parent or teacher
ImportantCheck the exact test used by your target schools

How to use the checklist

Do not turn this into a forty-question interrogation. Review one section at a time and ask for a quick example. If your child can sometimes complete a skill but needs a prompt, leave it unticked and add it to the action plan.

This is a preparation checklist, not a pass prediction. Grammar school tests differ by area, school and provider. Some include verbal and non-verbal reasoning; others place more weight on English and Maths. Always confirm the current admissions arrangements for each target school.

What the total means

Count the ticks out of 40, but also note which section contains the gaps. A child with 28 secure skills and weak timing needs a different plan from a child with the same score and significant Maths gaps.

0-16 secure

Prioritise core reading fluency, vocabulary and KS2 Maths. Delay heavy timed practice until the methods are more dependable.

17-29 secure

Choose the two weakest sections and teach those explicitly. Add short timed work once accuracy is consistent.

30-40 secure

Move towards mixed papers, unfamiliar questions, pacing decisions and careful analysis of small recurring errors.
